Before findings (source and user screenshot, not a browser audit):
- Purple-tinted backgrounds and decorative gradients competed with artwork.
- 24–32px frames and pill-shaped navigation repeated throughout the UI.
- Inset selection shadows created the curved border highlighted by the user.
- Panels were opaque; control borders and hard shadows added visual clutter.

Implemented:
- Achromatic #141414/#1c1c1c/#282828 surfaces, #8733ff primary with white labels.
- Neutral navigation selection, no inset bar, no resting button outlines.
- 6px controls / 8px inner surfaces / 12px outer panels. Round toggles remain semantic.
- SVG icons, system UI typography, no decorative starfield, orbit or glowing artwork.
- Floating panels use rgba(38,38,38,.92) + 24px backdrop blur, solid fallback and
  prefers-reduced-transparency handling; children are transparent to preserve the glass.
- Existing mobile layouts, focus indicators, picture pixels and research routes retained.

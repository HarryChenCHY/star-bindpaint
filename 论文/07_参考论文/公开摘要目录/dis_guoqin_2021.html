基于体验的4DVR交互媒体设计研究

Research of experience-based 4DVR interactive media design

郭　钦
关 键 词: 4DVR，设计原则，视觉保真度，飞行模拟，洗出算法
Keywords: 4DVR, design principles, picture fidelity, flight simulation, wash-out algorithm
摘 要
　　随着时代发展，娱乐和新技术的结合越来越紧密，而利用信息技术辅助实践教学也日益成为主要趋势。4DVR作为结合了4D座椅和VR的新技术，正在以上领域中发挥越来越重要的作用。但是目前4DVR交互媒体设计仍处在快速发展阶段，相关研究尤其是涉及用户体验的研究较为缺乏，市场上主流的4DVR交互媒体产品也仍然存在一些问题，尚未形成一个合理的、规范化的设计流程和设计原则，缺少相应的设计体系。适逢同济大学、上海航天技术研究院与徐州拓普技术研究院成立“沉浸式体感仿真实验室”，并展开飞行模拟相关课题的研究，笔者以飞行模拟为主题，对提升4DVR的用户体验的设计原则进行了研究。
　　本文一方面通过文献分析，一方面对目前的4DVR类虚拟仿真产品进行分析与总结，对4DVR用户体验方面存在的问题进行了归纳。笔者结合了视觉高保真度原则与虚实运动一致性原则，提出了4DVR交互媒体设计的设计原则，并基于该原则开发了《4DVR J15舰载机模拟训练系统》。最后，通过定量研究的方式，对提出的设计原则进行了分析论证，为今后的基于4DVR的交互媒体设计提供了指导意见，促进了该领域的发展与进步。
Abstract
With the development of the times, the combination of entertainment and new technologies is getting closer and closer, and the use of information technology to assist practical teaching is becoming a major trend. 4DVR is playing an increasingly important role in the above fields as a new technology that combines 4D seats and VR. However, 4DVR interactive media design is still in a rapid development stage, and there is a lack of relevant research, especially the research involving user experience, and there are still some problems with the mainstream 4DVR interactive media products in the market, which have not yet formed a reasonable and standardized design process and design principles, and lack a corresponding design system. As Tongji University, Shanghai Academy of Spaceflight Technology and Xuzhou Toptech Research Institute set up the “Immersive Body Simulation Laboratory” and started the research of flight simulation related topics, I studied the design principles of enhancing the user experience of 4DVR with the theme of flight simulation.
On one hand, through literature analysis, on the other hand, this paper analyzes and summarizes the current 4DVR-like virtual simulation products, and summarizes the problems in 4DVR user experience. The author combines the principles of visual high fidelity and virtual-real motion consistency to propose the design principles of 4DVR interactive media design, and develops the 4DVR J15 shipboard aircraft simulation training system based on the principles. Finally, the proposed design principles are analyzed and justified by means of quantitative research, which provides guidance for future 4DVR-based interactive media design and promotes the development and progress of the field.
